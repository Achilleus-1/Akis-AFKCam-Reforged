# Achilleus branding

- **Tagline:** Product of Achilleus.
- **Links and profiles:** https://linktr.ee/achilleus_
- **Brand color:** `#ba0239`.
- **Brand typeface:** Times New Roman, with Times or a similar serif as fallback.
- **Logo artwork:** original 2000 × 2000 RGBA PNGs supplied by Achilleus, copied without resizing, recoloring, or other alterations.
- **Minecraft Mods screen:** white logo, suitable for the default dark interface.
- **README:** black logo in light mode and white logo in dark mode; tagline rendered as an SVG with the brand color and serif font.
- The Minecraft settings interface continues to use Minecraft's native font. Brand typography applies to the documentation artwork.
- Original mod creator credits and license notices are retained separately from Achilleus's port and brand identity.

No SVG version of the logo is required for the current mod icons or documentation. A vector master is optional for future resizing or print use.
